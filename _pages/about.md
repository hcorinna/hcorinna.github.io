Hi there! I'm Corinna, a tech researcher at <a href="https://algorithmwatch.ch/en/" target="_blank">AlgorithmWatch CH</a> and a postdoctoral researcher in the <a href="https://www.ifi.uzh.ch/en/scg.html" target="_blank">Social Computing Group</a> at the University of Zurich. I coordinate <a href="https://algorithmwatch.ch/en/raisd/" target="_blank">RAISD – Researching AI, Society & Democracy in Switzerland</a>, a joint research project of the Social Computing Group and AlgorithmWatch CH that investigates how algorithms and AI affect society, public debate and democracy in Switzerland.

I completed my PhD in computer science at the University of Zurich and Zurich University of Applied Sciences in 2025, advised by <a href="https://www.ifi.uzh.ch/en/scg/people/hannak.html" target="_blank">Aniko Hannak</a> and <a href="https://www.zhaw.ch/en/about-us/person/heit/" target="_blank">Christoph Heitz</a>. My dissertation, <a href="https://doi.org/10.5167/uzh-434378" target="_blank">"A closer look: making sense of statistical fairness criteria through the lens of contemporary political philosophy"</a>, was part of the SNSF-funded project <a href="https://hcorinna.github.io/nrp77-fair-ai/" target="_blank">"Socially acceptable and fair AI"</a>.

I co-founded the European Conference on Algorithmic Fairness (ECAF, formerly the "European Workshop on Algorithmic Fairness", EWAF) and co-organized its <a href="https://2022.ewaf.org/" target="_blank">first</a> <a href="https://2023.ewaf.org/" target="_blank">two</a> editions. I'm now on the board of the <a href="https://eaaf.ewaf.org/" target="_blank">European Association for Algorithmic Fairness (EAAF)</a>, which steers the organization of ECAF.

Beyond research, I'm a representative of the <a href="https://www.vauz.uzh.ch/" target="_blank">VAUZ</a>, the association of junior academic staff at UZH: I serve as a deputy on the Senate, as a member of the Sustainability Commission and on the Mensaboard. I also co-founded <a href="https://linktr.ee/pbuzh" target="_blank">Plant-Based Universities Zurich</a> (<a href="https://www.instagram.com/pbu_zh/" target="_blank">@pbu_zh</a>), which is the UZH campaign of <a href="https://plantbaseduniversities.org/" target="_blank">Plant-Based Universities</a>. We demand that UZH goes 100% plant-based with its canteens and catering to <a href="https://www.global.uzh.ch/en/global_university/sustainable-internationalization.html" target="_blank">fulfill UZH's own sustainability goal of being climate-neutral by 2030</a>.

On weekends, there's a good chance you'll find me in the mountains, probably taking yet another picture of cows 🐮
