I've been re-elected to the board of the <a href="https://eaaf.ewaf.org/" target="_blank">European Association for Algorithmic Fairness (EAAF)</a> for the 2025–2027 term. Also, we renamed the "European Workshop on Algorithmic Fairness (EWAF)", which the EAAF steers, to "European Conference on Algorithmic Fairness (ECAF)" to better describe the event's actual structure and size. <a href="https://2026.ecafconference.org/" target="_blank">ECAF'26</a> takes place in Ghent on September 2–4, 2026.
