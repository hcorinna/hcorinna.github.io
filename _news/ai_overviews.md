Our first RAISD study is out: During the campaign on the "10 million" initiative, we collected more than 45,000 AI-generated summaries from Google and Bing and analyzed how often they appear and which sources they cite. You can read through the press release in <a href="https://algorithmwatch.ch/de/ai-overviews-und-die-10-millionen-initiative/" target="_blank">German</a> or <a href="https://algorithmwatch.ch/fr/initiative-des-10-millions-et-les-apercus-ia/" target="_blank">French</a> or <a href="https://algorithmwatch.ch/en/ai-summaries-on-the-10-million-initiative/" target="_blank">learn more about the methods we used (in English)</a>. The study was also covered by Swissinfo (<a href="https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/digital-democracy/how-googles-ai-generated-overviews-shape-swiss-democracy/91949683" target="_blank">English</a>, <a href="https://www.swissinfo.ch/ger/digitale-demokratie/so-beeinflussen-ki-%c3%bcbersichten-von-google-die-demokratie-in-der-schweiz/91879993" target="_blank">German</a> and <a href="https://www.swissinfo.ch/fre/democratie-numerique/quand-lia-sinvite-dans-le-d%c3%a9bat-d%c3%a9mocratique-suisse/91884246" target="_blank">French</a>).
